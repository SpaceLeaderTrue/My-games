# My-games


## Bali Loft

Walkable loft scene (WASD, mouse, or a phone joystick). Static copy of [bali-loft-live.surge.sh](https://bali-loft-live.surge.sh).

Open [`bali-loft/index.html`](bali-loft/index.html).

## SPACE — Cockpit

The original cockpit, plus a 3D chart you can fly through: stations and planets joined by routes. Same controls as before — WASD or arrows, Space or POWER for thrust, D / N / R on the gear lever.

Open [`space-cockpit/index.html`](space-cockpit/index.html).

## Искра — полёт птицы

Play online: [Искра](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/SpaceLeaderTrue/My-games/blob/cursor/bird-flight-game-f256/bird-flight/index.html)

Open-sky flight over a colorful country. Golden rings lead through a gorge, waterfalls above the forest, a rainbow lake, cloud peaks and a golden shore. The map stays open — leave the route whenever you want. A violet ring off to the side marks a twilight grove.

Open [`bird-flight/index.html`](bird-flight/index.html).

WASD or arrows steer, Space or a held mouse button flaps, Shift accelerates. On a phone, use the stick and the flap button. The minimap numbers the rings and draws a guide to the next one.
