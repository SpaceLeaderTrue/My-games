## Река → Океан

Play as a river finding its path to the ocean. Trailer-style 3D scene, Space Island branding throughout.

Open [`river-ocean/index.html`](river-ocean/index.html).

## Север — сектор Лиры

Cockpit flight through a small star system. Planets, a ringed giant, a comet, and six constellations move under gravity. Mouse looks, W/S is throttle, Shift or Space boosts, R kills speed, F toggles flight assist, G holds prograde.

Open [`space-cockpit/index.html`](space-cockpit/index.html).
