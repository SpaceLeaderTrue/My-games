## Wordmark
- Text: **SPACE** (Latin, all caps)
- Color: white `#FFFFFF` on black / dark
- Style: wide / extended geometric sans, **monolinear** stroke, **rounded corners** on every letter
- Aesthetic: minimalist tech / aerospace

### Letterforms (locked)
| Letter | Shape |
|--------|--------|
| **S** | Wide balanced curve, rounded ends, flat top/bottom segments |
| **P** | Stem + rounded bowl closing back to stem at mid-height |
| **A** | **HAS a horizontal crossbar (перекладина)** at mid-height joining the two slanted legs. Flat/slightly rounded apex. **Never** a barless Λ. |
| **C** | Wide open curve, rounded terminals |
| **E** | Spine + three horizontal bars (equal length; middle may be slightly shorter), rounded outer corners |

Optional ghost word **ISLAND** above SPACE (very dark / faint) in some lockups.

## Rules
- Prefer the official wordmark over any temporary island-circle mark
- **Do not invent a barless A** — the crossbar is part of the brand
- Product names (Island, River…) sit near the logo in UI type, not inside the wordmark
